About

Project Overview

AI Study Buddy is an AI-powered educational platform developed for IT-FESTIVAL 2026-27 to help students from Classes 6-10 learn through personalized explanations, textbook support, quizzes, diagrams, and performance analysis.

Future versions will include dedicated support for Classes 11-12 with stream-specific learning.

Purpose

Support students with fast, personalized study material for classroom learning and revision.

AI Capabilities

Generates explanations, diagrams, quizzes, flashcards, and performance insights from one workflow.
